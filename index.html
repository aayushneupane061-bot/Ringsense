Punch pattern

Move slowly. The prototype estimates arm-motion events; it cannot confirm a clean jab, cross or hook every time.

Guard & return

Between punches, bring both hands back toward cheek level without tensing your shoulders.

Stance & posture

Keep a comfortable staggered stance, knees soft, chin gently tucked and shoulders relaxed.

Balance

Rotate under control; avoid falling or reaching far beyond your base.

Footwork

Keep feet separated. Move in small steps and avoid crossing your feet. Full-foot visibility is required.

Coach cue: smooth and balanced first. Speed comes after control.
How accurate is this?

This is a prototype using browser pose landmarks and simple motion heuristics. Punch labels are tentative, and front-camera mirroring can complicate left/right interpretation. A single 2D camera cannot reliably measure force, impact, injury risk, exact rotation, or replace an experienced coach.

Safety: shadowbox only in a clear area. Do not hit walls, furniture, or people. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort. This app does not diagnose injuries or certify technique.
Privacy: this page processes camera frames locally in your browser; it does not send video to a RingSense server. The pose-model files load from a public CDN. Use only a trusted hosting provider.
